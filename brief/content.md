## Compare section

**Eyebrow:** Compare

**Heading (H2):** The differences that matter, side by side

**Body:** Opsvo highlights the attributes that separate your options and quietly drops the
ones that don't. Missing information is labelled as unavailable, never guessed.

**Label on the visual:** Illustrative example

This label is required. The doc's words: both the card and the table "must carry the label
'Illustrative example' so no one reads them as real listings or prices."

## FAQ section

Accordion, closed by default. One question open at a time is fine.

**What is Opsvo?** An AI shopping companion. You describe what you need, and it returns a
short list of suitable products with reasons, comparisons and sources.

**Does Opsvo buy things for me?** No, not without your approval. Opsvo hands you to the
retailer to complete your purchase. Any action that spends money or shares your details
needs your explicit confirmation first.

**Where do prices and product details come from?** From retailers and other sources Opsvo
can access. Each fact shows its source and when it was last checked. Prices and stock can
change, so the retailer's checkout is final.

**How are products ranked?** By how well they match the requirements you gave.
